Update a procedure

Choose the procedure, enter its new approved revision and select the replacement PDF, Word or text file. Future checks will use that version; existing reports stay unchanged.

Local work instructions (optional)

Add approved local instructions as additional context for future reviews. They do not replace default procedures or enable AI review of Trainer Review areas. Files are stored by the background helper; relevant text is sent to MDT-GPT with a review. Upload PDF, Word, text, Excel (.xlsx/.xlsm) or CSV files. Save older .xls files as .xlsx or CSV first. Up to 20 instructions, 20 MB per file. Saved reports stay unchanged.

Connection settings
