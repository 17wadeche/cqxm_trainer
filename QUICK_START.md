# Check my work — for trainees

1. Open your product event in GCH and save your changes as usual.
2. Click **Check my work** at the top right.
3. If prompted, paste your **MDT-GPT Production API token** and click **Continue**. Select **Remember** to protect and save it for your Windows account. Production, GPT-5.6-terra and Bearer authentication are already set.
4. Wait while the tool downloads the Detailed Event Report and checks relevant sections against the six training procedures. PESR is the fallback when the detailed report is unavailable.
5. Click **Open Word report**. A copy is already in Downloads.

The report contains a concise findings table covering all 13 areas and up to five study priorities. Share it with your trainer. Full evidence remains saved separately.

No separate application, pairing step, manual complaint upload or model selection is required. You can collapse the panel while it runs. To update a procedure, use **Documents & settings**, choose its approved revision and save the replacement.

When upgrading from the earlier provider, enter a Production token once when asked. Updating from version 0.5.1 to 0.6.0 preserves a successfully remembered Production token. Existing procedure replacements remain available.

If setup or a review fails, give the displayed message to your trainer. A rejected gpt-5.6-terra model needs the MDT-GPT owner to confirm Production access. A network error needs IT to check `api.gpt.medtronic.com:443`. Use the security settings required by Medtronic; the tool does not disable Zscaler or certificate verification.

For installation or upgrades, give your trainer INSTALL_FOR_TRAINER.md.
